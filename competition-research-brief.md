# AutoTrade — competition research brief

AutoTrade is an autonomous, LLM-supervised momentum-trading application for Alpaca and Interactive Brokers (IBKR). A deterministic scheduler continuously ingests broker account and position state plus market prices and bars, calculates technical features including EMA, RSI, and ATR, and emits risk- and signal-based events such as entries, stop breaches, drawdowns, take-profit conditions, stale data, and daily-loss breaches. An LLM Trader consumes only structured evidence and tools to evaluate opportunities and manage positions, while a separate Concierge gives the human operator a persistent conversational interface. The system combines policy-controlled position sizing, correlation/exposure controls, macro-regime adjustments, broker-fill reconciliation, append-only decision/fill journals, outcome scorecards, and a durable lessons loop so decisions and measured outcomes survive individual agent cycles.

AutoTrade is designed as a safety-first operating layer rather than an unconstrained trading chatbot. It keeps hard risk checks below the LLM at the order-execution boundary—valid stops, buying-power and position-limit checks, daily-loss limits, duplicate-position prevention, and policy ceilings that the agent cannot modify. Paper trading can run autonomously, while live execution is derived from the actual broker endpoint and is protected by a deterministic operator approval gate immediately before an order is sent. The current product submits market orders and tracks internal stop/target baselines, while exposing broker orders and protective-order coverage to the operator. Competitor research should cover AI trading agents and trading copilots, algorithmic-trading platforms, broker-integrated strategy automation, portfolio/risk operating systems, and backtest-to-live execution frameworks—especially products that combine autonomous decision-making, human oversight, explainability/audit trails, and retail/prosumer brokerage connectivity.
